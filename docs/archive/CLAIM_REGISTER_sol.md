# Claim register and evidentiary decisions

The authoritative decision table is `CLAIM_REGISTER_sol.csv`. It applies the
prospective rules in `EVIDENTIARY_STANDARD_sol.md` after the parameter
centralization and full rerun.

## Decision summary

- **Class A retained:** the central within-region year-1 SOC-buffering direction,
  canonical S3 trajectories, calibration outputs, zero-shock stationarity and
  the regenerated cross-region diagnostics.
- **Class B retained with explicit conditions:** SC1/SC2, the four-region partial
  budget, the fixed-ceiling N-capture sensitivity, the joint-prior ensemble and
  the threshold-sensitive country mechanism screen.
- **Class C retained only as benchmarks:** Broadbalk, the 2022 demand hindcast
  and OFRA. None is described as a validation of the full coupled model.
- **Class D excluded:** the old four-pool quantitative comparison, all-region
  financial rankings, the old 57% N-capture result and any claim that the
  year-10 SSA SOC gradient is robust to unsupported negative soil-N demand
  elasticities.

The principal conclusion survives the gate: higher SOC reduces the immediate
yield penalty of a fertilizer shock within every modeled region. The gate
narrows three secondary claims: financial outcomes are limited to four audited
price pairs; N capture is a conditional sensitivity rather than a quantified
intervention benefit; and long-run SSA monotonicity is a central-model result,
not robust over the full unsupported soil-N demand-elasticity grid.
