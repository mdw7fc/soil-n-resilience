# Frozen evidence for F-022 through F-025

Diagnostics computed against the PRE-F-025 model (linear market clearing).
They measure the gap between realized and econ-implied production, its
decomposition, the price bias under two output concepts, and the prototype
before/after comparison that justified adopting realized clearing. They are
point-in-time evidence for FINDINGS.md entries F-022, F-023 and F-024, not
regenerable artifacts: rerunning their generators against the current model
would measure a gap the model no longer has. Frozen 2026-08-29.
